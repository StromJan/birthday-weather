# Was it stormy on your birthday?

A small web page I made for students. You type in your birthday and it shows what the winds looked like over Northern Europe on that day. The idea is to get people curious about weather maps by starting from a date that means something to them.

The page works in English and Finnish (toggle in the top right of the card).

## How it works

There are two data sources to choose from:

- **Ventusky** (1979 onwards): the map opens right on the page, showing 10 m wind at 12 UTC. You can switch layers in the map to look at temperature, rain, pressure and so on.
- **ERA5** (1940 onwards): opens the Copernicus Weather Replay in a new tab with wind gusts, mean sea level pressure and wind particles. Useful if the birthday is before 1979, e.g. for teachers or grandparents.

Both maps are centred on Helsinki, but you can pan around freely.
